E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dataset

Source

- The original sales dataset was sourced from Kaggle.
- The downloaded dataset was used as the starting point for building the database for this project.

Data Preparation

- The original dataset was analyzed and reorganized to identify the different entities involved in the e-commerce business.
- Customer and order information was separated into dedicated customers and orders tables.
- Product-related information was separated and organized into a dedicated products table.
- A separate sellers table was created to represent seller information.
- Additional data was incorporated where required to make the database more complete for analysis.
- The data was cleaned and structured to maintain consistency across the tables.
- Appropriate relationships were established between customers, orders, products, and sellers using their respective IDs.
- The resulting tables form a connected relational e-commerce database suitable for SQL analysis.

Final Dataset

- Customers: Customer information including name, signup date, and city.
- Orders: Order information including order date, product, quantity, price, total amount, order status, payment method, and city.
- Products: Product information including product name, category, unit price, and seller.
- Sellers: Seller information including seller name and city.
- Orders: 10,000 records.
